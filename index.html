Pipelineclips

Pipelineclips is an internal publishing tool that turns gaming livestream highlights into short vertical videos and publishes them to our own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

How it works

  1. We select highlight clips from streamers who allow clipping of their content.
  2. Each clip is edited into a 9:16 short with subtitles, a hook and a visible credit to the original streamer.
  3. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) once and authorizes Pipelineclips to publish videos to that account.
  4. Pipelineclips uploads the finished video via the TikTok Content Posting API. The account owner reviews the caption, privacy level and interaction settings before posting.

Content rights

We only process content from streamers who allow clipping (official clipping programs, public permission or direct consent). Every video credits the streamer in the video and caption. Takedown requests are honored immediately — contact us at alfredjurgens274@gmail.com.
